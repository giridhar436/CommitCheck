# Production Page & UX State Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive, evidence-based audit of CommitCheck against production readiness standards, covering legal disclosures, customer lifecycle flows, and system UX states. Every determination is anchored in repository code, Prisma schema definitions, Next.js route handlers, and architectural records (ADRs).

## 3. Information That Must Never Be Invented (Consolidated Open Section)

The following operational and legal facts are required for full commercial legal publication but cannot be guessed from repository evidence:

1. **Legal Operating Entity**: Registered company name (e.g., CommitCheck Inc. / CommitCheck LLC), jurisdiction of incorporation, registered business address.
2. **Official Legal & Privacy Contact**: Formal privacy email address (e.g., `privacy@commitcheck.dev`), Data Protection Officer (DPO) details, or legal mailing address.
3. **Security Vulnerability Reporting Address**: Dedicated security inbox (e.g., `security@commitcheck.dev`) or PGP key for vulnerability disclosure.
4. **Governing Law Jurisdiction**: The specific state, country, or legal jurisdiction for dispute resolution (e.g., Delaware, California, England & Wales).
5. **Support SLA & Channels**: Official customer support email (e.g., `support@commitcheck.dev`) and live support operating hours.

*Notice*: All legal, policy, and support pages created in this initiative strictly describe the verified technical functionality of CommitCheck (OAuth scopes, Gemini 1.5 Pro AI analysis, Recall.ai bot joins, Linear syncing, Supabase PostgreSQL storage) without fabricating imaginary company registrations or fake compliance certifications. Contact details use standard configurable placeholders (`support@commitcheck.dev`, `privacy@commitcheck.dev`, `security@commitcheck.dev`) with prominent notices that operator details must be filled in prior to commercial launch.
